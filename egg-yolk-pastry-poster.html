What Is It

A Golden Treasure Inside Flaky Layers

Egg yolk pastry (蛋黄酥, dàn huáng sū) is a beloved Chinese pastry featuring a golden salted egg yolk wrapped in sweet red bean paste, encased in dozens of paper-thin flaky layers. Each bite is a perfect balance of salty, sweet, and buttery — a masterpiece of Chinese baking.

How It's Made

The Art of Layering

1
Make the Doughs 制作水油皮与油酥
Prepare two doughs: a water-oil dough and a pure butter-flour pastry for the flaky layers.
2
Wrap & Roll 包酥擀卷
Wrap the pastry dough inside the water dough, then roll and fold repeatedly to create dozens of thin layers.
3
Fill with Treasure 包入馅料
Place a salted egg yolk in the center of a red bean paste ball, then wrap it all inside the layered dough.
4
Brush & Sprinkle 刷蛋液撒芝麻
Brush with egg wash for a golden sheen, then top with black sesame seeds.
5
Bake to Perfection 烘烤至金黄
Bake at 180°C (350°F) for 25 minutes until golden and beautifully cracked, revealing the flaky layers.

Cultural Significance

More Than Just a Pastry

🌕
Moon Festival Favorite: Egg yolk pastries are especially popular during the Mid-Autumn Festival (中秋节). The round golden yolk symbolizes the full moon — a symbol of reunion and togetherness.
🏠
Family Reunion: Sharing egg yolk pastries is a way to express love and longing for family. The act of baking them at home has become a cherished tradition across generations.
🎁
The Perfect Gift: Beautifully boxed egg yolk pastries are one of the most popular gifts during Chinese festivals — a gesture of warmth, respect, and good wishes.
