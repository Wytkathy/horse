02 · Language & Culture

Horse Idioms to Impress

Chinese has dozens of idioms (成语) featuring horses. Swipe through these popular ones — perfect for New Year greetings!

马到成功
Mǎ dào chénggōng
"Success upon the horse's arrival"
Achieve success right from the start. Like English "hit the ground running" — the #1 Horse Year blessing!
龙马精神
Lóng mǎ jīngshén
"Spirit of the Dragon-Horse"
Boundless energy and vitality. Dragon + Horse = the ultimate power combo in Chinese mythology.
一马当先
Yī mǎ dāng xiān
"One horse takes the lead"
To be a trailblazer, the first to charge ahead. Originally described a brave cavalry rider leading the troops.
万马奔腾
Wàn mǎ bēnténg
"Ten thousand horses galloping"
A scene of unstoppable momentum and power. Used to describe prosperous, thrilling times.

03 · The Special Year

2026 = "Red Fire Horse" 🔥

Not all Horse years are alike! China uses a 60-year grand cycle combining 10 Heavenly Stems × 12 Earthly Branches. Each year has its own element and color.

丙
Bǐng · Fire 🔥
+
午
Wǔ · Horse 🐴
=
红马年
Red Fire Horse

Both the stem (丙) and branch (午) carry Fire energy — making 2026 a rare double-fire year! This only happens once every 60 years. The last one was 1966.

🧭 Beyond time: "午 (Wǔ)" also means due south on the compass and noon on the clock (11am–1pm). The Chinese word for "meridian" is 子午线 — "子 (North) + 午 (South) Line"!

05 · Traditions

The "Wear Red" Custom 🔴

In your Běn Mìng Nián (本命年) — your zodiac year that returns every 12 years — Chinese tradition says you're more vulnerable to misfortune. Your zodiac guardian is said to return to heaven for a "performance review," temporarily leaving you unprotected!

The solution? Wear red everywhere. Red is the ultimate lucky color in Chinese culture.
